Privacy Policy

RTY (Rolled Throughput Yield)

Overview

Last updated: October 8, 2026

RTY ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Collection

RTY does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data You Enter

The process steps and figures you type into the app, and any process you choose to save, are stored locally on your device only. This data is never uploaded, synced to a server, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

Device Permissions

RTY does not request access to your camera, microphone, speech recognition, photo library, location, contacts, or any other protected part of your device.

Reports and Exports

PDF reports and CSV data exports generated by the app are created on your device and saved or shared to a location you choose (such as Files or Mail), under your control. The developer has no access to these files.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full version is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment details.

Third-Party Services

RTY does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing SDKs.

Children's Privacy

RTY does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, as no data is collected at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
