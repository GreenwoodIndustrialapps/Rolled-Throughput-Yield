About RTY

35 years of chasing hidden losses on the shop floor, built into an app.

A career in quality

I've spent 35 years in quality, Six Sigma, and Lean within automotive manufacturing, and one lesson keeps coming back: a process can look fine step by step and still be losing a surprising amount along the way. Every station reports a yield in the high nineties, the final inspection passes most of what reaches it, and yet a lot of good effort goes into sorting, fixing and redoing work that a simple final yield figure never shows.

The gap

Rolled throughput yield is the honest way to see that, because it multiplies every step together and counts only what passes first time. But working it out meant a spreadsheet, and even then the next question, which step is really costing us the most, took more effort again. It was never quite at hand standing on the shop floor with the numbers from the last shift.

Why RTY

So I built the tool I wished I'd had, as a companion to Process Capability App and Scatter Plot. List your steps and enter whatever figures you have for each one, whether that is units in and out, a pass rate, or a Cpk, and the app gives you the rolled throughput yield and a normalised yield you can compare across processes of different lengths.

From there, a Pareto of yield loss shows the few steps responsible for most of it, a Funnel lets you watch units drop out of your process, and a What-if tab lets you try improvements and work backwards from a target RTY to see which steps would need to improve, and by how much.

Everything runs entirely on your own device. Nothing you enter ever leaves your phone, which matters in a manufacturing environment as much as it does anywhere else.
